Zeph Schafer

Zeph Schafer leads data engineering at MANTL, a fintech company building modern banking infrastructure for credit unions and community banks. He has spent the past several years building a data system from the gound up, which now manages data for hundreds of financial institutions across the United States on a streaming architecture that processes hundreds of terabytes daily .

Before MANTL, he helped venture-backed startups build scalable data warehouses. He holds an MSc in Economic History from the London School of Economics and a BSc in Economics from the University of Oregon.
